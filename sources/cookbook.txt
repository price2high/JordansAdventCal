DAY 01: Soft-Baked Holiday Gingerbread Cookies (or
Sugar Cookies)
Card Border: Royal Stewart Plaid | Paired Healing Drink: Spiced Hibiscus-Ginger Sparkler
(Tip: For classic Holiday Sugar Cookies on any version, omit molasses and spices and add 1
extra tsp vanilla.)
1. The Classic
●
Ingredients: 1/2 cup unsalted butter (softened), 3/4 cup dark brown sugar, 1/3 cup
molasses, 1 egg, 1 tsp vanilla, 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour, 1 tsp baking soda, 1/2 tsp
sea salt, 2 tsp ginger, 1 1/2 tsp cinnamon, 1/4 tsp cloves, 1/4 tsp nutmeg. Glaze: 1 cup
powdered sugar, 2 tbsp heavy cream, 1/2 tsp vanilla bean paste.
●
Method: Cream butter and brown sugar 2 minutes; beat in molasses, egg, and
vanilla. Fold in whisked dry ingredients. Scoop 2-tbsp balls onto parchment and bake
at 350°F for 9–11 minutes. Cool and drizzle with whisked glaze.
2. The Sculpt (Weight-Loss)
●
Ingredients: 1 1/2 cups almond flour, 1/2 cup vanilla whey-casein protein powder,
1/3 cup brown monk fruit sweetener, 1/4 cup 0% Greek yogurt, 2 tbsp melted
butter, 1 1/2 tbsp blackstrap molasses, 1 egg white, 1 tsp vanilla, 1/2 tsp baking soda,
1/4 tsp salt, 2 tsp ginger, 1 1/2 tsp cinnamon, 1/4 tsp cloves. Glaze: 1/4 cup
powdered monk fruit, 2 tbsp Greek yogurt, 1 tsp almond milk.
●
Method: Whisk wet ingredients smooth, fold in dry ingredients, press into 1/2-inch
rounds on parchment, and bake at 350°F for 8–10 minutes. Cool and glaze.
3. The Nourish (Healthy Weight-Gain)
●
Ingredients: 1/2 cup grass-fed butter or ghee, 1/3 cup creamy almond butter, 1/2
cup coconut sugar, 1/4 cup maple syrup, 1/4 cup blackstrap molasses, 1 egg + 1 yolk,
1 3/4 cups oat flour, 1/2 cup whole wheat pastry flour, 1/4 cup hemp hearts, 2
scoops collagen peptides, 1 tsp baking soda, 1/2 tsp salt, 2 tsp ginger, 1 1/2 tsp
cinnamon, 1/4 tsp cloves. Glaze: 1/4 cup mascarpone, 2 tbsp maple syrup, 1/2 tsp
vanilla.
●
Method: Beat butter, almond butter, sugars, and molasses; mix in egg and yolk. Fold
in dry ingredients. Scoop 3-tbsp mounds and bake at 350°F for 10–12 minutes. Swirl
with glaze.

=== PAGE 3 ===
4. The Pure (Alkaline / Vegan)
●
Ingredients: 2 cups spelt flour, 1/2 cup date sugar, 1/3 cup date syrup or blue agave,
1/3 cup melted walnut or coconut oil, 2 tbsp sea moss gel, 2–3 tbsp walnut or hemp
milk, 2 tsp ginger, 1 1/2 tsp Ceylon cinnamon, 1/4 tsp cloves, 1/4 tsp sea salt. Glaze: 3
tbsp melted coconut butter, 1 tbsp agave, 1 tbsp warm walnut milk.
●
Method: Whisk oil, date syrup, sea moss gel, and milk. Stir in dry ingredients. Flatten
into 1/2-inch discs on parchment and bake at 350°F for 10–12 minutes. Drizzle with
warm glaze.
Healing Pairing: Spiced Hibiscus-Ginger Sparkler
●
Benefits: Hibiscus supports healthy blood pressure and antioxidants; ginger aids
digestion; Ceylon cinnamon steadies blood sugar.
●
Recipe (Serves 2): Simmer 2 cups water with 1-inch sliced fresh ginger, 1 Ceylon
cinnamon stick, and 1 star anise for 8 minutes. Off heat, steep 3 tbsp dried hibiscus
petals for 10 minutes. Strain, sweeten lightly with raw honey or agave if desired,
chill, and top with 1 cup sparkling mineral water over ice with rosemary and
cranberries.
DAY 02: Sparkling Candied Cranberries
Card Border: Black Watch Tartan | Paired Healing Drink: Rosemary-Cranberry Digestive
Tonic
1. The Classic
●
Ingredients: 2 cups fresh whole cranberries, 1 cup water, 1 cup white sugar (for
syrup), 1 cup superfine sugar (for rolling), 1 tsp vanilla extract.
●
Method: Simmer water and 1 cup sugar until dissolved; stir in vanilla and cool 5
minutes. Toss cranberries in syrup and refrigerate 6 hours. Drain, dry 1 hour on a
rack, and roll in superfine sugar until frosted.
2. The Sculpt (Weight-Loss)
●
Ingredients: 2 cups fresh cranberries, 1 cup water, 1/2 cup allulose (syrup), 1/2 cup
powdered monk fruit-erythritol blend (coating), 1 tsp orange zest.
●
Method: Simmer water, allulose, and zest; cool slightly, soak cranberries 4 hours,
drain well, dry 1 hour, and toss in powdered monk fruit.

3. The Nourish (Healthy Weight-Gain)
●
Ingredients: 2 cups fresh cranberries, 3/4 cup pure maple syrup, 1/4 cup raw honey,
1/2 cup finely chopped toasted pecans, 1/2 cup shredded unsweetened coconut, 1/4
cup coconut sugar.
●
Method: Warm maple syrup and honey; coat cranberries and chill 4 hours. Drain and
roll in a mixture of coconut sugar, finely chopped pecans, and coconut flakes for a

4. The Pure (Alkaline / Vegan)
●
Ingredients: 2 cups fresh cranberries, 3/4 cup raw blue agave, 1/2 cup spring water,
1/2 cup finely ground date sugar, 1/2 tsp Ceylon cinnamon.
●
Method: Warm agave and water; soak cranberries 4 hours. Drain, dry 1 hour on
parchment, and roll in date sugar mixed with Ceylon cinnamon.
Healing Pairing: Rosemary-Cranberry Digestive Tonic
●
Benefits: Cranberries support urinary and gut health; rosemary relieves bloating;
raw apple cider vinegar aids digestive enzymes.
●
Recipe (Serves 2): Steep 2 fresh rosemary sprigs and 1/4 cup crushed fresh
cranberries in 1 1/2 cups hot water for 10 minutes. Strain, chill, add 1 tbsp raw apple
cider vinegar (or key lime juice for alkaline) and 1 tsp agave/honey, and top with
sparkling water.
DAY 03: Cinnamon Apple Chai Scones
Card Border: Burnished Gold-Foil Frame | Paired Healing Drink: Golden Turmeric-
Cardamom Milk
1. The Classic
●
Ingredients: 2 cups all-purpose flour, 1/3 cup brown sugar, 1 tbsp baking powder,
1/2 tsp salt, 2 tsp chai spice (cinnamon, cardamom, ginger, clove), 1/2 cup cold
unsalted butter (cubed), 1 cup diced Honeycrisp apple, 1/2 cup heavy cream, 1 egg, 1
tsp vanilla. Glaze: 1 cup powdered sugar, 2 tbsp brewed chai tea.
●
Method: Cut cold butter into dry ingredients until crumbly. Fold in apples, then mix
in cream, egg, and vanilla. Shape into an 8-inch disc, cut into 8 wedges, brush with
cream, and bake at 400°F for 18–20 minutes. Drizzle with chai glaze.

DAY 04: Warm Baked Stuffed Apples
Card Border: Warm Linen Texture | Paired Healing Drink: Fennel & Roasted Cinnamon Tea

1. The Classic
●
Ingredients: 4 Honeycrisp apples (cored), 1/2 cup rolled oats, 1/3 cup brown sugar,
1/4 cup melted butter, 1/4 cup chopped pecans, 1 tsp cinnamon, 1/4 tsp nutmeg, 1/2
cup apple cider (for pan).
●
Method: Mix oats, brown sugar, butter, pecans, and spices. Pack into cored apples.
Place in a baking dish with apple cider at the bottom, cover with foil, and bake at
375°F for 35 minutes; uncover 10 minutes until tender.
2. The Sculpt (Weight-Loss)
●
Ingredients: 4 apples (cored), 1/2 cup quick oats, 2 tbsp brown monk fruit, 1/4 cup
vanilla Greek yogurt, 1 tbsp light butter, 2 tbsp chopped pecans, 1 tsp cinnamon, 1/2
cup water + 1 tbsp lemon juice.
●
Method: Mix filling, stuff apples, bake in water/lemon bath at 375°F for 40 minutes.
Top with an extra dollop of Greek yogurt.
3. The Nourish (Healthy Weight-Gain)
●
Ingredients: 4 large apples (cored), 1/2 cup oats, 1/3 cup almond butter, 3 tbsp
ghee, 1/4 cup maple syrup, 1/2 cup chopped walnuts and pecans, 2 tbsp chia seeds,
1 tsp cinnamon.
●
Method: Combine rich nut-butter oat filling, pack generously into apples, bake at
375°F for 40–45 minutes, and serve with full-fat coconut cream.
4. The Pure (Alkaline / Vegan)
●
Ingredients: 4 organic apples (cored), 1/2 cup quinoa flakes or crushed walnuts, 4
chopped Medjool dates, 2 tbsp melted coconut oil, 2 tbsp date syrup, 1 tsp Ceylon
cinnamon, 1/4 tsp ginger, 1/2 cup spring water.
●
Method: Stuff apples with date-walnut-oil mixture, bake in a dish with spring water
at 375°F for 40 minutes until caramelized.
Healing Pairing: Fennel & Roasted Cinnamon Tea
●
Benefits: Fennel seeds act as a natural prebiotic and relieve gas/bloating; cinnamon
warms circulation.
●
Recipe (Serves 2): Lightly toast 1 tbsp fennel seeds and 1 Ceylon cinnamon stick in a
dry pot for 1 minute until fragrant. Add 2 cups boiling water, steep 8 minutes, strain,
and sip warm.

DAY 05: Heirloom Holiday Apple Pie
Card Border: Royal Stewart Plaid | Paired Healing Drink: Warm Chamomile-Apple Cider
Toddy
1. The Classic
●
Ingredients: 1 double flaky butter pie crust, 6 cups peeled sliced Granny Smith &
Honeycrisp apples, 3/4 cup brown sugar, 1/4 cup white sugar, 3 tbsp all-purpose
flour, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 2 tsp cinnamon, 1/2 tsp nutmeg, 2 tbsp cold butter (cubed),
1 egg (for wash).
●
Method: Toss apples with sugars, flour, lemon, and spices. Pour into bottom crust,
dot with butter, weave lattice top crust, brush with egg wash, and bake at 400°F for
20 minutes, then 350°F for 35–40 minutes until bubbling.
2. The Sculpt (Weight-Loss)
●
Ingredients: Crust: 2 cups almond flour, 3 tbsp melted butter, 1 egg white, 2 tbsp
monk fruit. Filling: 6 cups sliced apples, 1/2 cup brown monk fruit, 2 tbsp arrowroot
or cornstarch, 2 tsp cinnamon, 1 tbsp lemon juice.
●
Method: Press almond crust into pie dish and par-bake 8 minutes at 350°F. Fill with
spiced apples, top with a light oat-almond crumble (1/2 cup oats, 2 tbsp monk fruit,
1 tbsp butter), and bake 35–40 minutes.
3. The Nourish (Healthy Weight-Gain)
●
Ingredients: Whole wheat butter pie crust, 6 cups apples, 1/2 cup maple syrup, 1/3
cup coconut sugar, 3 tbsp tapioca flour, 1/2 cup toasted pecans, 3 tbsp grass-fed
butter, 2 tsp cinnamon, 1/4 cup heavy cream drizzled inside before topping.
●
Method: Assemble with double crust or thick pecan-oat streusel, bake at 375°F for
50 minutes until deep golden.
4. The Pure (Alkaline / Vegan)
●
Ingredients: Crust: 2 cups spelt flour, 1/2 cup cold coconut oil, 2 tbsp date sugar, 4–
6 tbsp ice water. Filling: 6 cups sliced apples, 1/2 cup date sugar, 1/4 cup agave, 3
tbsp arrowroot starch, 2 tsp Ceylon cinnamon, 1/2 tsp ginger, 1 tbsp key lime juice.
●
Method: Roll out spelt crust, fill with spiced apples, top with lattice or spelt-walnut
crumble, and bake at 375°F for 45–50 minutes.

DAY 07: Classic Vanilla Bean Ice Cream
Card Border: Burnished Gold-Foil Frame | Paired Healing Drink: Peppermint-Cacao Nib
Infusion
1. The Classic
●
Ingredients: 2 cups heavy whipping cream, 1 cup whole milk, 3/4 cup sugar, 1 vanilla

vanilla bean paste, pinch of salt.
●
Method: Blend all ingredients until completely smooth. Freeze in a Ninja Creami pint
or churn in an ice cream maker for 20 minutes for a high-protein soft serve.
